KoeNaWin for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Privacy at a glance: KoeNaWin is an offline-first app. It does not require an account, does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not automatically send your practice information to the developer.

1. Information stored on your device

KoeNaWin stores your selected start date, counting progress, reminder preferences, appearance preference, and related app settings locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app’s features.

The developer cannot access this locally stored information. You can remove it by resetting progress inside the app or deleting the app from your device.

2. Data collection and tracking

KoeNaWin does not automatically collect, sell, rent, share, or transmit personal information, practice history, device identifiers, location, contacts, advertising information, or analytics data to the developer.

The app does not use third-party advertising, tracking, crash-reporting, or analytics SDKs.

3. Notifications

If you enable reminders, KoeNaWin requests notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device. Reminder scheduling does not send your practice information to the developer. You can disable notifications at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

4. Calendar access

Calendar integration is optional. If you enable it, KoeNaWin requests permission to access calendars available through Apple’s EventKit framework and lets you choose a calendar.

The app adds only the KoeNaWin stage start dates, stage end dates, and vegetarian days to the calendar you select. Calendar information is handled by iOS and your chosen calendar provider. KoeNaWin does not send calendar contents to the developer.

You can disable calendar sync in the app and manage calendar permission in iOS Settings.

5. Backup and export

KoeNaWin can export progress as a JSON file and import a backup selected by you. Exporting, saving, sharing, or storing that file is under your control. The app does not upload backups to a developer-operated server.

6. Support email

If you choose to contact support, the app opens your email application. Your email address, message, and any information you voluntarily include are sent only when you choose to send the email. Support correspondence is used to respond to your request and may be retained as reasonably necessary for support, security, or legal obligations.

7. Children’s privacy

KoeNaWin does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require an account and does not automatically transmit personal data to the developer, no child profile is created by the app.

8. Security and your choices

Your practice information is protected by the security features of your Apple device. You control notification and calendar permissions through iOS Settings and may stop using or delete the app at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.

10. Contact

KoeNaWin is developed and maintained by Steeve. For privacy questions or support, email yekhaung@bazelinks.com.
